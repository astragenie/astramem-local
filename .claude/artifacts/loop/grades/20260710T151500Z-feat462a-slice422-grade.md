## Lessons

- Deterministic seeded sampling makes scope-narrowing INVISIBLE: a smaller n
  under the same seed is a strict subset, so a mis-flagged resume replays
  everything from ledger and reports success. Persist scope params and refuse
  on mismatch — warn is not enough when the output is a published number.
- Single shared decision function (evaluateResumeGuard) consumed by both CLIs
  made the guard unit-testable in milliseconds AND provably identical across
  harnesses — validator confirmed same behavior live on both.
- Validation scenario 2 accidentally reproduced the original incident
  (orchestrator kill takes daemon down) and the fix held: exactly-once proven
  by the daemon's own ingestCallCount, not inferred from client logs.
