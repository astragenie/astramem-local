## Lessons

- StdioServerTransport (MCP SDK) never listens for stdin 'end' — a stdio
  server entry must drive its own shutdown (server.close → db.close →
  exit 0). Verified against vendored SDK source, worth remembering for any
  future stdio-based tool.
- The smoke sandbox helpers (makeIsolatedDaemonEnv) already redirect
  APPDATA/LOCALAPPDATA/HOME/XDG_* + datadir together — reusing them made
  the new e2e isolation airtight for free.
- Builder hit its session limit AFTER finishing and committing — reports
  arrived intact. Front-load commit before final report when limits loom.

## Decisions

### DEC-003: mcp-stdio ships unadvertised (no --help/README entry)

**Rationale**: The subcommand exists to enable transport-level testing; it is
deliberately not daemon-grade (no instance lock, no crash handlers, no job
recovery). Advertising it would invite production use it is not hardened for.

**Consequences**:
- positive: e2e coverage of the MCP surface with a minimal new public surface
- negative: discoverability is low; promotion to a documented surface later
  requires adding the lock + FEAT-409 dim preflight (backlog note, not filed)

## Surprises

- vitest's describe.sequential false-deprecation flag from the IDE tsserver —
  actual vitest 4.1.9 type defs carry no @deprecated tag; tsc clean.

## Followups
