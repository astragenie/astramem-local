## Lessons

- Char-scanner replacements for regex need grammar-positional rules: treating ANY quote as state-open regressed odd-apostrophe unquoted values (don't/O'Brien) — quotes only open at value-start positions.
- Boot-smoke validation of a "pure parse" change still paid: /health degraded status proved the parsed baseUrl was genuinely used, not silently defaulted.

## Decisions

### DEC-010: Value-start-only quote grammar for the hand-rolled loader

**Rationale**: Mid-value apostrophes are literal characters in YAML unquoted scalars; only a quote at a legal value-start position opens quote state. Keeps the no-YAML-library stance while making comment stripping quote-aware.

**Consequences**:
- positive: ` #` now legal inside quoted scalars and flow-sequence entries; apostrophe regression closed; FEAT-461 customPatterns caveat removed
- negative: nested flow-sequence arrays remain unsupported by the fallback splitter (disclosed; no array-of-arrays Config field exists)

## Surprises

- The regression fired only on ODD apostrophe counts — even counts self-corrected, which is exactly the kind of coincidence that slips past casual fixtures.

## Followups

- Nested-bracket flow-sequence fallback corrupts silently — becomes relevant only if an array-of-arrays Config field ever ships.

## What went well

Two-round review with adversarial value-start attacks; validator boot smoke gave three-way independent evidence (parse call, doctor resolver, live daemon).

## What went wrong

Round-1 scanner shipped a corruption regression the old regex was immune to by construction — new fixtures mirrored the ACs but not prose-with-apostrophe reality.

## What I would do differently next time

When replacing a dumb-but-safe mechanism with a smarter one, enumerate what the dumb one could NEVER get wrong and fixture those cases first.
